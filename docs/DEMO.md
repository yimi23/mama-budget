# The demo. The judge drives. About 60 seconds.

Before judging: `npm run seed`, `npm run api`, extension loaded, Target.com cart open and empty, the judge's iPhone number in `api/.env` as PHOTON_TO (the Mac kit texts from this Mac, no signup; `GET /photon/health` must say `sender: imessage`). Rehearse with `PHOTON_DRY=1 npm run api` so nothing is sent; see docs/PHOTON.md.

1. **Rice and soap.** Judge adds both. Badge stays calm. Say: "Rice at Target. Her gele stays low. She reads the item, not the store."
2. **AirPods.** Judge adds them. Badge shakes, card opens, gele climbs, she speaks. Say nothing. Let the room hear her.
3. **Buy anyway.** Judge taps it. Charge posts to Nessie, gele comes down, their iPhone buzzes with her text. Say: "That charge is now on a Capital One Nessie account. And she texted."
4. **Send $50 home.** Judge texts her "send 50 home." She moves it in Nessie and replies. Nothing scolded. Then Proud. Say: "Money to family is never waste. That rule is hard coded."
5. **The score.** One slide. "On 50 purchases labeled by three mothers, Mama agreed with them x of N times. The bank's category map got y. She never scolded a need z times out of N." Say the real numbers, whatever they are.

Beat 2b. Ask the judge to name a store. Open it, add something. She appears. Say nothing about how.

If Target.com breaks: switch to the Amazon tab. Same extension, same Mama. Do not explain, just switch. If Amazon breaks too, the extract layer takes over from the page text; it is slower, keep talking.
If the API is down: restart it. Nessie down does not matter, the cache carries it.
